The Row That Answers Your Question
Somebody in your market already decided the calendar was the problem
Heaven's Best Cedar City publishes: /fall-landing-page/ /holiday-landing-page/ /landing-page-spring/ /landing-summer/ Easy Breezy Pros publishes: nothing seasonal at all

You told Paige your goal is consistency through winter. A national franchise operating in your town has already built a page for fall and a page for the holidays.

And they are doing it without any analytics we can find, for carpet cleaning only — which is the summer service. They have no air duct page and no dryer vent page at all.

So the winter ground — ducts, dryer vents, indoor air quality, fire safety — is completely uncontested in this market. Nobody has written a word about it. You have the only three service pages that cover it, and the only 59 blog posts, and you go quiet in December.

And they did it badly, which is the opportunity

Two of their twelve URLs are misspelled — carpet-cleaqning-paragonah and panquitch for Panguitch. All twelve are carpet only. Not one of them mentions air ducts or dryer vents, because they do not publish those services.

Your site already names St George, Hurricane and Washington as areas you serve. The St George page exists and is built correctly — it just never got repeated, and it only covers one of your three services.

Where You Are Already Winning
This is worth saying plainly, because it changes what the work should be
  • 59 blog posts against nobody. Heaven's Best publishes no blog. Duct Pros has one post and it is still the default WordPress “Hello world”.
  • You are the only firm here with any analytics or advertising tag we could find. You can see what works and they cannot.
  • You are the only one with all three services published — air duct, dryer vent and carpet. Heaven's Best has carpet only; Duct Pros has no carpet.
  • Duct Pros still has sample-page and hello-world live in their sitemap, and no enquiry form anywhere we could find. Heaven's Best has six test pages published.
  • You have two review pages and a certifications page. That is real trust infrastructure.

You are not losing this market on quality. You are the strongest site in it. Which is exactly why the answer to a quiet winter is not “work harder on the website” — it is the calendar, the towns, and the commercial buyers none of you are chasing.

And where you are genuinely exposed

Stanley Steemer and SERVPRO both serve Cedar City. We could not read their local pages properly — both are large JavaScript-driven national sites — so we make no claim about what they publish here. But they carry national brand recognition and national ad budgets, and SERVPRO in particular owns the restoration and insurance channel, which is the most valuable commercial work in your trade.

Neither of your two local rivals has a Meta pixel. Neither do you. That is the one row where nobody in this market is doing the work, and it is the cheapest advantage available.
