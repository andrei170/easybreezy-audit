Launch Call — 30 September 2026. What We Do First, What We Need From You, And When Each Piece Goes Live.

Prepared for Ben Hansen · AG1 Consulting · Following the agreement of 26 September 2026

Two things are broken before anything else can work, and they are the first two things on this list.

Your Meta ad account is restricted, so you cannot run targeted advertising at all. Google is holding your profile over licensing you have already proven. Neither is your fault and neither is chargeable.

Both have review queues we do not control, which is why they start today rather than in week two.

Five items needed from you • Four weeks to fully live • Fall page first, because the season is already here
What We Need From You On This Call

Nothing here takes more than a few minutes, but the two review queues cannot start until the first two are done. If we get these on the call, week one starts today.

  • Admin access to your Facebook Business Manager You
    Not the page — the Business Manager behind it. We need it to open a clean ad account and appeal the restriction on the old one. Without this the Meta problem cannot begin.
  • Manager access to your Google Business Profile You
    So we can resubmit the Utah licensing documentation in the format Google actually accepts. You have already proven this once; it was submitted the wrong way.
  • Website login — hosting or CMS, whichever gets us to the files You
    Needed for the pixel, call tracking, schema markup and every new page. If you are not sure what you have, tell us who built the site and we will work it out.
  • Domain / DNS login You
    Only needed if we have to verify a domain with Meta or add records. Ten minutes, once.
  • Your service area, in your own words You
    Which towns you will actually drive to and which you turn down. The location pages are only worth building for places you want the work from.
The one thing that will decide how fast this moves

The Meta restriction appeal and the Google licensing review are both queues at somebody else's company. We can submit them properly and chase them. We cannot make them decide.

That is why they go in first. Everything else on this plan is work we control and can schedule. These two are the only items with a waiting period, so they start on day one.

The Four Weeks
Week 1
Unblock, then measure. New Facebook page and clean ad account opened, restriction appealed on the old one. Google licensing resubmitted with the Utah documentation. Meta pixel installed and firing — properly this time, so events are actually recorded. Call tracking live. By the end of week one you can see what your website does. You cannot today.
Week 2
The fall page, because the season will not wait. A seasonal landing page built and live, and dryer vent moved up the site — it is your strongest service and it is barely on your homepage. LocalBusiness and service markup added so search engines can read what you do.
Week 3
Town and location pages. You have one service-area page covering everywhere. We build a page per town you actually serve, which is how people in those towns find you. Holiday and winter seasonal pages drafted ready to publish.
Week 4
Review, then decide about advertising. First full month of measurement in hand. We look at what the pages did before spending anything. Ads are a separate decision and a separate fee, and we would rather you made it with a month of your own numbers in front of you.
Why the fall page comes before the town pages

It is already the end of September. A fall page published in week two gets the season; the same page in week four gets half of it. Town pages compound slowly and will still be compounding in January.

Order matters more than volume here. Same work, same four weeks, and doing it in this sequence is worth more than doing it in any other.

Two things we are deliberately not doing

We are not charging you to fix the Facebook and Google problems. Those are somebody else's mess and undoing them is not a service we would invoice for.

We are not starting ads in week one. Your account is restricted so we could not anyway, but even once it is lifted we would rather spend a month measuring first. Advertising into a site you cannot measure is how the last lot spent your money.

What We Are Not Promising
  • No date on the Meta restriction being lifted. We will appeal it properly and chase it. The decision is Meta's and appeals can take weeks.
  • No date on the Google licensing review either, for the same reason. What we can promise is that it goes in correctly, which is what did not happen before.
  • No ranking or enquiry numbers. You have had a year of a site nobody could measure. The first month is about being able to see, not about a figure we made up in advance.
  • No January promise. You told us last January produced one appointment. The plan is built so a month like that costs you less rather than more — that is a pricing structure, not a forecast.
