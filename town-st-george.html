Why now

The season is here, and so is the risk.

The US Fire Administration looked at clothes dryer fires in homes. Two numbers from its report matter at this time of year.

“Clothes dryer fire incidence in residential buildings was higher in the fall and winter months, peaking in January at 11 percent.”

US Fire Administration

“Failure to clean (34 percent) was the leading factor contributing to the ignition of clothes dryer fires in residential buildings.”

US Fire Administration
Source: US Fire Administration, clothes dryer fires in residential buildings, based on 2008 to 2010 National Fire Incident Reporting System data. Figures are national, not specific to Utah.
