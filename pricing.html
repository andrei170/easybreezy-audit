Below ten jobs a month, Option B is cheaper. Above ten, Option A is. They cross at exactly ten.

Being straight about the arithmetic, because you can do it yourself

Our management fee normally starts at a thousand a month. Your entire ad budget is about a thousand a month. Paying us the same as you spend makes no sense and we are not going to pretend otherwise.

So we would start you well below that and move it up as the spend grows, which is something we have done before and is exactly the case the sliding scale exists for. The number gets set against your spend, not against a price list.

And we are not starting it now. Right now your Meta account is restricted and there is no pixel, so nobody — us included — can tell you what that spend is producing. Paying anyone to manage ads you cannot measure is money set on fire.

The order is: fix the tracking, watch it for a few weeks, then have this conversation with real numbers in front of us. What we said on the call stands. We want to see you make more money before we ask for more.
